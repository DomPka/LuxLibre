Control your flashlight from this page

Brightness, battery, profiles and settings - over Bluetooth, no app to install.

This browser cannot use Bluetooth

  • Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS: open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • iPhone and iPad: install the free Bluefy browser from the App Store and open this page in it. Safari and Chrome on iPhone cannot do this.
  • Firefox and Safari do not support Web Bluetooth at all.

You can still look around with Try demo mode below.

  1. Wake up the flashlight's Bluetooth. Switch the light on - or, if it is off, click the button three times quickly. The small colour LED blinks blue.
  2. Tap Connect and choose the device whose name starts with Aurora- in the list your browser shows.

Settings

Lock the flashlight

Stops it switching on by accident, for example in a bag. To unlock, turn this off here or click the button four times.

Find my flashlight

The small colour LED blinks cyan three times.

Keep Bluetooth on

To save power the flashlight switches Bluetooth off after a while. This keeps it available for a few more minutes. Tap again to extend.

Profile

A profile decides how bright each level is and how hard the battery and the LED are pushed.

Make it stick

The flashlight only remembers the profile and brightness level you picked after you save them.

Edit the Custom profile

Apply switches the flashlight to your Custom profile right away. Save to flashlight also stores it, so it is still there after the battery was removed or the light was off for a long time.
