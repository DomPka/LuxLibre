# Code of conduct

LuxLibre is built by people with very different backgrounds: students and engineers,
hobbyists and companies, many languages and countries. We want everyone to feel welcome to ask,
measure, build and disagree.

## We expect

- Respect and patience, especially with beginners. Every expert once asked the obvious question.
- Discussion of ideas and data, not of people. Disagree with an argument, not with a person.
- Good faith: assume that others want the project to succeed.
- Honesty about uncertainty: say "I measured", "I estimated" or "I don't know".
- Safety first: point out risks clearly and kindly.

## We do not accept

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Sharing other people's private information without their permission.
- Spam, deliberate disruption, and pressure to bypass the safety rules.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and temporarily or permanently
exclude people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately
through the repository's contact options, or open an issue if the matter is not sensitive.
Reports are handled confidentially.
