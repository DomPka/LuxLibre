Connect a light

Read the status of a flashlight with the LuxLibre firmware, change its settings and choose the colours of its colour beam. For the ESP32-C3 lights with a USB-C port: EDCO, EDCO Mini and EDCO Prism.

Uses Web Serial: Chrome or Edge on a computer, with a USB-C data cable. Phones and tablets cannot do this.

This browser cannot connect to a light. It has no Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS). The demo works here.

The light does not show up
  • Use a USB-C cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.
  • Tick Show all serial ports and look for a USB JTAG/serial port.
  • Close other programs and tabs that use the port, such as a serial monitor or the firmware installer.
  • On Linux your user may need to be in the dialout group.
  • The light needs the LuxLibre firmware 0.4 or newer.

The light keeps working while it is connected, and its button always has priority. Changes take effect at once; Save to the light keeps them after a restart. Everything runs in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

All settings

A change is sent when you leave the field or press Enter. The light uses it at once and keeps it after a restart only once you save.

Put all settings back to their defaults? The calibration (BAT_CAL, THM_OFFSET) is kept. The light uses the defaults at once; save to keep them.

Log (the last 50 lines)

    1 change not saved. The light forgets it when it restarts.

    Safety: lithium cells, bright LEDs and hot surfaces can hurt people. Some settings change the protection against heat and a flat cell. Read the safety notes and the parameter list before you change them.
