In your browser

These pages run entirely in your browser: nothing is uploaded and there is no account. Each has a demo mode, so you can try it without a light.

  • LuxLibre settingsRead and change every setting of a light with the LuxLibre firmware over USB-C, and pick the colours of the EDCO Prism. Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  • FlashKa control appBattery, temperature, levels, profiles and the presenter mode over Bluetooth. Chrome or Edge on Android and computers; Bluefy on iPhone and iPad.
  • FlashKa firmware installerWrites the firmware through the light's USB-C port and checks it. Chrome or Edge on a computer, with a USB-C data cable.
